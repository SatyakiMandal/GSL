Summary

This report examines Adani Enterprises (ADANIENT.NS) between 20 January 2023 and 17 February 2023, covering 20 trading days. It collects what the Indian financial press published about the company over that period, measures how the stock moved relative to ^NSEI, and identifies days where notable coverage and an unusual company-specific price move happened to fall together.

Price moves are reported as abnormal returns: the company's actual return minus what would have been expected given the benchmark's move that day, using a market model fitted on price history from before this window. This separates the company's own move from the market's, so a day when everything fell is not mistaken for company-specific news.

Across 132 collected items of coverage, 4 candidate days met both tests. The most prominent was 27 January 2023, with an abnormal return of -17.12% alongside negative coverage.

Of the 4 flagged days, 2 showed a price move in the direction the coverage's tone would suggest and 2 did not. Days where the two disagree are kept in the report rather than filtered out, because they are often the more interesting cases.
